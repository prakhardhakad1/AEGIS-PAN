Judge Requirement #4 // Scientific Honesty & Negative Results

What Didn't Work: Calibration Breakdowns, Small-Cohort Failure (`TCGA-KICH`) & Observational Confounding

4 Documented Negative Findings
1. Where Empirical-Bayes Shrinkage Hurt (`TCGA-GBM` Extreme Drift)

While Empirical-Bayes shrinkage (γ_EB) reduced 5-year calibration error in 8 of 9 cohorts, it performed worse than unregularized MLE in Glioblastoma (`TCGA-GBM`: EB error 3.4 pts vs. Naive MLE 2.9 pts). Because `TCGA-GBM` has an extreme baseline hazard (5-year cancer mortality 78%, median follow-up 12.0 mos, held-out O/E = 1.38), shrinking its local multiplier toward the pan-cancer prior (γ = 1.00) introduces pull-toward-the-mean bias.

2. Small-Cohort Instability (`TCGA-KICH`, N = 113, ~12 Events)

Kidney Chromophobe (`TCGA-KICH`, N = 113, longest median follow-up 48.0 mos) achieves high discrimination (LOCO C = 0.798), but with fewer than 15 primary cancer deaths its held-out 5-year O/E = 0.78 has an unacceptably wide 95% confidence interval (0.41 to 1.48) and fails events-per-variable (EPV < 10) requirements for standalone Royston-Parmar spline fitting.

3. Cross-Organ Baseline Calibration Failure Without Recalibration

In Leave-One-Cohort-Out (LOCO) transport without an organ-specific baseline anchor, pooled 5-year O/E spans from 0.78 (`KICH`) and 0.86 (`BRCA`) up to 1.38 (`GBM`), with between-organ heterogeneity I² = 88.4%. Stage and age rank patients reliably within an organ, but absolute 5-year mortality requires organ- and hospital-specific baseline hazard calibration.

4. Observational Adjuvant Therapy aSHR = 0.75 Is Not Causal

The adjuvant therapy subdistribution hazard ratio (aSHR = 0.75, 95% CI 0.66–0.85; aRMTLd = −4.15 mos, CLEP.S521309) is derived from multivariable Fine-Gray & pseudo-value RMTL regression adjusted for age, T-stage, N-stage, grade, and receptor status. Because clinicians selectively prescribe adjuvant therapy to patients with better performance status, unmeasured confounding by indication remains.
