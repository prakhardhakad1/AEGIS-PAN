Module 03 // Cause-Specific Cox (csHR) vs. Fine-Gray Subdistribution (sHR) & Follow-Up Cutoff Lab

Why Naive Kaplan-Meier & Short Follow-Up Cutoffs Distort Cancer Risk (PMC7953595 & PMC11698617)

In solid-tumor cohorts, 25.4% of all-cause deaths arise from competing non-cancer causes (incidence 6.2%, CLEP.S521309). Treating competing deaths as simple right-censoring in a standard Kaplan-Meier curve systematically overestimates cancer mortality (1 − KM bias), while applying arbitrary 5-year vs. 8-year binary cutoffs (TREMSUCS-TCGA, PMC11698617) misclassifies censored survivors.
